Welcome

Music that feels like part of the moment, not a soundtrack to it.

I'm Faith Gladstone, a classically trained violinist serving Alpharetta and the greater North Atlanta area. Whether it's a wedding ceremony, a cocktail hour, or a private lesson, I bring the same care and attention to every note — tailoring the music to the people and the occasion.

I split my work between two things I love: performing live for weddings and events, and teaching violin to students of all ages. Explore both below, or reach out directly if you have something in mind.
