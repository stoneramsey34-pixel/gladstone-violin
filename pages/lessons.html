Suzuki Method

Private Violin Lessons

I'd love to help you (or your little one!) fall in love with the violin. I'm classically trained and teach using the Suzuki method — a listening- and repetition-based approach that builds a strong musical ear right alongside solid technique. I welcome students of all ages, and I have a special love for beginners and intermediate beginners, helping you build confident fundamentals from your very first lesson. Curious whether lessons are a good fit? Send me a message and let's talk!
