Weddings & Events

Wedding Packages:

Choose the package that fits your day, or reach out to build something custom.

Ceremony

$600

Amplified violin, accompanied with piano backing tracks for a fuller sound. 30 minute prelude, processional, recessional, and 1-2 songs as guests leave. We will work together to customize your set list from songs you choose from my repertoire and up to three song requests not included in my repertoire. Travel fee may apply depending on location.

Cocktail Hour / Dinner Hour

$600

One hour of background music with a custom set list based on songs you choose from my repertoire and up to three song requests not included in my repertoire. Amplified violin, accompanied with piano backing tracks for a fuller sound. Travel fee may apply depending on location.

Ceremony & Cocktail

$1000

Ceremony package and cocktail package combined. Travel fee may apply depending on location.

Special Projects

Studio music, special performances with chamber groups, orchestras, bands, etc. Inquire for your custom package!
