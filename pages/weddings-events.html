Weddings & Events

Live Violin, Tailored to You

Music that fits the moment.

From the first note of the processional to the last song of the night, I work with couples and event planners to build a soundtrack that feels personal — not generic. That means real conversations about the songs that matter to you, the mood you're going for, and the flow of the day.

I perform for wedding ceremonies and cocktail hours, corporate and private events, and special projects — solo violin, or paired with other musicians depending on what your event calls for.

  • Wedding ceremonies
  • Cocktail hour & reception music
  • Private & corporate events
  • Special projects & custom collaborations
