About Faith

Hi, I'm Faith.

[Placeholder bio copy] I'm a classically trained violinist based in Alpharetta, Georgia, with a lifelong love of music that started long before I could reach the top of the fingerboard. [Placeholder bio copy] My training is rooted in classical technique, but I care most about connection — making sure the music fits the moment, whether that's walking a bride down the aisle or helping a beginner play their first full song.
